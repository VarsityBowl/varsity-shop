Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Varsity Bowl ("we", "our", "us") operates the website located at sportfusion.online. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our site.

1. Information We Collect

Varsity Bowl is designed to be privacy-first. We collect minimal information:

a. Automatically Collected Information

  • Anonymous analytics: We may collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable information such as page views, browser type, device type, and general geographic region (country/state level). This is used strictly for improving the service.
  • Server logs: Our hosting provider (CloudFlare) automatically logs IP addresses for security and abuse prevention purposes. These logs are not accessible to us in individually identifiable form.

b. Information You Provide

  • Email: If you contact us via email at adminmodis@gmail.com, we will receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.
  • Local storage: We may store preferences (such as your filter selections) locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require account registration to use Varsity Bowl.
  • We do not collect names, passwords, or payment information.
  • We do not use tracking pixels or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Varsity Bowl uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase / Firestore: Stores schedule data used to display games. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • CloudFlare Pages: Static hosting for this website. CloudFlare Privacy Statement
  • Tailwind CDN / jsDelivr / unpkg: Content delivery networks for styling and JavaScript libraries. These may log requests per their standard CDN practices.

4. Data Sources

Game schedules displayed on Varsity Bowl are aggregated from publicly available high school athletics sources. We display factual information (team names, dates, times, locations) that is publicly available and not personally identifiable to any individual.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Varsity Bowl is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at adminmodis@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your information.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at adminmodis@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect the limited data we handle, including HTTPS encryption and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Schedule data is retained only as long as necessary. Game data is automatically purged from our database approximately 3 days after the game takes place. Email correspondence is retained for up to 2 years unless you request earlier deletion.

9. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage (not cookies) for storing user preferences such as filter selections. We do not use third-party cookies for advertising or tracking.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Email: adminmodis@gmail.com
