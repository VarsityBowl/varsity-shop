Common Questions

How do I report an incorrect schedule?
Email us with the game details (teams, date, incorrect info) and the correct information. We'll update our database.
Can I request a team to be added?
Yes! Send us the MaxPreps team URL and we'll add it to our scraping rotation.
How do I partner with Varsity Bowl?
Send a partnership proposal to our email. We're open to sponsorships, cross-promotions, and content collaborations.
Found a bug on the website?
Please email us with: (1) what you did, (2) what you expected, (3) what happened. Screenshots help!
DMCA / Copyright issues?
See our Terms of Service section 10 for the formal DMCA process.
