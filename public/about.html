About Varsity Bowl

The Hub for High School Sports.

Our Mission

Varsity Bowl is a free, independent platform that brings together the best high school sports matchups across the United States. From Friday Night Lights to state championship week, we curate the games that matter most for fans, families, scouts, and athletes.

We cover 15 sports — Football, Basketball, Baseball, Softball, Soccer, Volleyball, Wrestling, Track & Field, Cross Country, Lacrosse, Water Polo, Tennis, Golf, Swimming, and Field Hockey — across all 50 states.

What We Do

  • →Curate Prestigious Matchups: We rank games by prestige score based on matchup quality, rivalry intensity, and postseason stakes.
  • →Aggregate Schedules: Real-time scheduling data from publicly available athletic sources.
  • →Enable Discovery: Filter by sport, state, team, or league to find the games you care about.
  • →Provide Watch Access: Direct links to live streaming platforms for gameday viewing.

Independence & Transparency

Varsity Bowl is an independent network. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any high school, school district, or athletic association.

All game information (team names, schedules, locations) is factual, publicly available information. Team logos and school names belong to their respective owners and are used for identification purposes only under fair use.
